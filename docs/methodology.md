# Editorial & Privacy Policy

This policy applies to every page in this repository and on its website.

1. **First-person record.** This site documents one employee's contributions and experience. Statements about others are the owner's account and opinion, not findings.
2. **Labels.** Every personal statement is marked My recollection, Company's written response, Disputed or Awaiting documentary verification, as defined on the employee experience page.
3. **No invention.** No facts, dates, documents, quotations or legal findings are added that the owner cannot support.
4. **Privacy.** Not published: confidential company information, internal reports, customer data, other employees' personal details, private emails, salary slips, phone numbers.
5. **People.** Individuals are referred to by role, not name, unless naming is necessary and approved.
6. **Corrections.** Evidence-backed corrections are made promptly and recorded in the changelog.
7. **Right of reply.** The company and anyone referred to may respond.
8. **Approval.** Nothing is published without the owner's explicit approval.
9. **Contribution status.** Professional contributions and figures are marked **Pending documentary verification** until they are confirmed. They are grouped as work described as completed, work in progress, or proposed work. Work described as completed also remains pending documentary verification.
10. **Contact details.** No personal contact details, including email addresses, appear on any page until the owner approves them.
11. **Period.** Only 11 May 2026 – 24 October 2026 is covered. Earlier employment is not included. The exception is the personal story page, which may mention limited earlier professional background as context; the employee experience record and the dispute it describes remain limited to 11 May – 24 October 2026.
12. **Fair representation.** Where a material statement is disputed, the record will distinguish the owner's account from the company's response. Any response or correction will be presented accurately and without misleading edits.
