## Account

### 1. Rejoining — *My recollection; awaiting documentary verification*

I rejoined Japan Electronics on 11 May 2026 following discussions with management and assurances given to me. Based on that decision, I left my job in Karachi, moved from Karachi, and arranged accommodation in Islamabad.

### 2. My role — *My recollection; awaiting documentary verification*

During this period I worked in business intelligence and strategic planning. My functional profile was Head of Business Intelligence & Strategic Planning; this is not a claim of my official designation, which is not independently confirmed through an appointment letter.

My work covered management reporting, KPI and MIS dashboards, target-setting frameworks, branch, inventory and marketing analysis, incentive reporting, and reporting automation. The full list is on the [professional contributions](../contributions/) page, where every item is marked Pending documentary verification.

I also built TA Leads, a combined tool covering recruitment tracking, team leads tracking and online sales leads tracking.

### 3. PIP / warning documentation — *My recollection; disputed*

I was presented with a PIP / warning document. I refused to sign it.

My recollection is that it was presented to me while I was giving a presentation of my regular Business Intelligence work and of TA Leads, the additional tool described in section 2. At that time, the Head of Department, Online Team, was appreciating the tool. I record these as the circumstances in which the document was presented to me; I do not state that the presentation and the PIP / warning were connected.

My recollection is that the date initially stated in connection with it was the 26th, and that approximately one month later HR / GM referred to the 25th. I raised this date discrepancy. I do not state an exact document date, and this account has not been checked against any document.

### 4. Separation — *My recollection; disputed*

My employment ended on 24 October 2026.

During the final-settlement paperwork, the resignation option was marked under "Other". My position is that I was pressured in this process.

The company's written explanation is summarised in section 5. It is presented separately from my account of pressure during the final-settlement process. These are two separate accounts, and neither is presented here as an established finding.

### 5. The company's explanation — *Company's written response; awaiting documentary verification*

In an email dated 12 November 2026 from the company's HR department, the company:

- cited broader restructuring and cost optimisation as the reason for the decision;
- stated that the decision was final;
- stated that dues had been settled and cleared.

This is my summary of the company's written response; the email itself is not reproduced here. I dispute that my dues and documents have been fully settled (see section 6).

### 6. Settlement and documents — *My recollection; awaiting documentary verification*

I requested:

- final settlement details;
- my salary slips for the last two months;
- a clearance certificate / NOC;
- an experience / service certificate;
- the return of my original security cheque.

My current objective is compensation and these employment-related documents.
